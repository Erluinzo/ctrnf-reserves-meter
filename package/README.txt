Reserves Meter 1.1.0 - by Onur

Shows your boost reserves in the race HUD: seconds left ("12.4") and a bar,
under the lap counter. Works with every boost bar style.
Bar: green 0-10 s, red 10-20 s, blue-white 20-30 s.
In Time Trial Replay Mode it shows the watched ghost's reserves.

One local player. Allowed online.
Game: CTR Nitro-Fueled 1.21 on the OCTR launcher. This ZIP is for USA
(CUSA13795); the -eu ZIP is for EU (CUSA14876). Enable only yours.
Install: Mods > Add files, choose the ZIP, enable it, restart the game.

1.1.0: EU edition; allowed online.
