# Reserves Meter

Shows your boost reserves in the race HUD of CTR Nitro-Fueled ([OCTR PC port](https://github.com/Ryu7w7/OCTR-NitroFueled-PC)):
seconds left (`12.4`) and a bar right under the turbo bar (moved up a little), in every boost bar style.
Bar: green 0-10 s, red 10-20 s, blue-white 20-30 s.

One local player. Allowed online. In Ghost Viewer it shows the watched ghost's reserves; in a [Replay Mode](https://github.com/Erluinzo/ctrnf-replay-mode) replay, the watched racer's (1.1.1).

## Install
Download the ZIP for your game from [Releases](https://github.com/Erluinzo/ctrnf-reserves-meter/releases):
`reserves-meter` (USA, CUSA13795) or `reserves-meter-eu` (EU, CUSA14876), version 1.21.
In the launcher: Mods > Add files, enable it, restart the game.

## Build
Needs the OCTR checkout at `../CTRNF`, gcc and binutils: `python3 build.py`.
